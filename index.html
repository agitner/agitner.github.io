About

Academic profile

This site brings together core information that would usually appear on an academic profile page: a short biography, research themes, current projects, selected publications, and ways to get in touch.

Adam Gitner’s recent work includes directing the digitization of Corpus Glossariorum Latinorum Online and publishing on methods for naming and indexing Latin texts in the Thesaurus linguae Latinae.

Research

Areas of focus

Latin lexicography

Research centered on the Thesaurus Linguae Latinae and the description of Latin texts, vocabulary, and sources.

Digital classics

Building and refining digital scholarly resources for ancient texts, with an emphasis on searchable corpora and sustainable research data.

Text indexing & source description

Methods for organizing Latin source material, naming texts, and connecting traditional philology with computational workflows.

Projects

Selected work

Digital corpus project

Corpus Glossariorum Latinorum Online (CGLO)

A digitized and curated online edition of Goetz’s Corpus Glossariorum Latinorum, including corrected markdown, XML, and JSON data for the Thesaurus glossarum.
